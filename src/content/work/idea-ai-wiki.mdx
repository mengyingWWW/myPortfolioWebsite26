As the only designer, I audited it across four core flows, found 15 UX gaps, and redesigned each flow around two users: a technician writing at a GED reading level, and an AI that needs structure to answer from. <Section id="insights" index="04" label="Insights" title="Insights">

  <ol>
    <InsightRow
      cause="Unstructured"

    />
    <InsightRow
      cause="Untraceable"
      count={7}
      insight="You couldn’t tell who wrote a page, who could see it, or what had changed."
      principle="Owned at a glance."
      text="Author, audience and history show on every page."
    />
    <InsightRow
      cause="Uncategorized"
      count={4}
      insight="Tags were optional, so nobody filled them in. The AI routes on tags."
      principle="Suggest, then confirm."
      text="The tool proposes tags. People accept them with a click."
      accent
    />

    <Takeaway n={1} title="Human first in AI product design">The wiki has two kinds of users: the people who write and read it, and the AI agent that answers requests from it. I designed for the people first, the technicians, reviewers, admins and requesters, many with little time at a computer. Only then did I ask how the agent would use the content. Solving the human problems gave the AI most of what it needed.</Takeaway>
    <Takeaway n={2} title="Audit before redesign">This project started from an engineering draft that already worked, so my first job was to understand it. On a three-person team, I reported directly to the CEO and worked with the engineer who built both the AI agent and the web app. The audit set a clear direction for the redesign and gave all three of us the same list of gaps to discuss.</Takeaway>
    <Takeaway n={3} title="A solo designer’s workflow with AI">As the only designer, I had no one to critique ideas with. A chat assistant became that sounding board, and Figma Make let me build clickable prototypes without waiting on engineering. The engineer got working flows instead of static screens. One rule kept it on track: I set the direction from the audit, and checked every AI-generated change against it.</Takeaway>
