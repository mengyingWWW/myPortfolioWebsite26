<Section id="takeaways" index="09" label="Takeaways" title="Takeaways">
  <ol>
    <Takeaway n={1} title="Rethinking my role and workflow with AI">Working with AI changed what my job looked like. I skipped a full set of wireframes. I sketched the base frame by hand, and Figma Make turned it into a hi-fi prototype. That hi-fi was far from done. My time went into the rounds after it, judging each change and refining details until it was ready to hand off. I didn’t go back and redraw it as static mockups, because the clickable prototype already served as the spec. Most of my value was in deciding what stays.</Takeaway>
    <Takeaway n={2} title="Comparing options at a startup’s pace">AI prototyping made it fast to build two or three versions of an open question. I put them side by side in the weekly review, and the CEO and engineer chose between things they could click. That kept decisions moving at a startup’s pace.</Takeaway>
    <Takeaway n={3} title="Learning from proven patterns">Mature products already solve parts of this problem. Airbnb groups crowded listings into clusters, and indoor maps let people switch floors. I studied why each pattern works and what it assumes about the people using it. The real work was fitting it to a school campus, where one room can hold dozens of assets and floors have names like B1 or M.</Takeaway>
  </ol>
</Section>
