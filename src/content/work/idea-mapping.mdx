summary: "Guardian is IDEA’s AI-powered facilities-management platform. Its records say where every asset, person and work order should be. On site, technicians, admins and responders still walk the halls to find them. Mapping is the new mobile feature that puts those records on a map. I started from an engineering spec: five pin types, four use cases and two map layers, with open questions about floors, crowded pins and modes. As the sole designer, I answered them in an interactive prototype that served as the spec. With it, the engineer built the feature in a third of the planned time, and the core design has shipped."
