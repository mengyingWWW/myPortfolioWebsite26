<Section id="insights" index="04" label="Insights" title="Insights">
  <p class="max-w-[680px] text-lg leading-[1.6] text-muted">The 14 issues came from three causes.</p>
  <ol>
    <InsightRow
      cause="Unclear AI role"
      count={5}
      insight="Teachers couldn’t tell what the AI would make, or how to change what it made."
      principle="Show the AI’s part."
      text="Every field says what the teacher adds and what the AI adds. AI output stays editable."
      accent
    />
    <InsightRow
      cause="Broken hand-offs"
      count={5}
      insight="Each feature works on its own. Teachers got lost moving between them."
      principle="Carry the work forward."
      text="A new rubric attaches itself. Edit and gradebook settings sit where teachers look for them."
    />
    <InsightRow
      cause="Not like Canvas"
      count={4}
      insight="All five teachers grade in Canvas. They looked for a Save button and their district’s terms."
      principle="Match habits they already have."
      text="Confirm every save. Use the rating scales districts use."
    />
  </ol>

<Section id="takeaways" index="07" label="Takeaways" title="Takeaways">
  <ol>
    <Takeaway n={1} title="Walk every role before the first session">We wrote the tasks from the teacher’s side and missed the “Include in Gradebook” toggle ourselves. All five teachers missed it too. We caught it when a student account showed no grades. Now I run each task end to end, from every role, before testing.</Takeaway>
    <Takeaway n={2} title="Pilot with someone who matches the user">Our pilot participant didn’t closely match our teachers. The script and questionnaire changed after the first sessions, so early sessions ran a little differently. My next pilot will be a working teacher.</Takeaway>
    <Takeaway n={3} title="What five sessions can’t tell us">Five teachers show where people get stuck. They can’t show how often it happens. Math teachers also found the AI Grader less useful than English and social studies teachers did, so subject may matter. A next round would test the fixes with more teachers per subject and track task completion.</Takeaway>
  </ol>
